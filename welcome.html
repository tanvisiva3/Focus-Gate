Welcome to Focus Gate!

Focus Gate blocks distracting sites until you answer study questions correctly.

  1. Choose your site. You can add or remove sites in Settings.
  2. When you visit a blocked site, instead of loading, it shows you a quiz.
  3. Pick a subject and answer. Get enough right and the site unlocks for a set amount of time.
  4. It locks again. When the time is up, the site is blocked again, even if the tab is still open.

Tip: click the puzzle piece icon in your toolbar and pin Focus Gate to check your lock status any time.
